## Most likely rejection and exact fix

The most likely rejection is not simply “authored is not physical.” It is:

> The positive benchmark is generated by a reconstruction/segmentation pipeline closely related to the quantities used to choose the gate, while the only naturally occurring real event is a null result. The fixture record itself calls the data teacher-rendered, exploratory, and “not evidence about the physical world” ([absence-fixture-lane:7–16](/D:/adags/research-wiki/operations/absence-fixture-lane-2026-09-10.md:7)). Exact workshop-grade fix:

1. Replace “genuine full-multiview absence” everywhere with “authored counterfactual full-multiview absence.”
2. Re-score the existing renders with independently adjudicated masks that were not used for construction or membership.
3. Present the SAM 2 arm only on its admitted subset, including the sear refusal and flame timing failure.
4. If time permits, freeze one fresh scene/window before any results and construct, estimate membership, and evaluate it with disjoint models/annotations.
5. Use this limitations sentence verbatim or equivalently:

> “Our evidence establishes behaviour on segmentation-guided counterfactual removals in captured multiview footage; it does not establish automatic discovery of physical scene exit or performance on naturally occurring absence-and-return events.”

That limitation is acceptable at a workshop. Omitting it is not.

## Frozen NOT_MET versus 12/12

Reporting both is honest only if the frozen result leads and the 12/12 result is explicitly called post hoc/descriptive. The record is unambiguous: Claim A was `NOT_MET` on both confirmatory scenes; one G–U P1 result was +0.44 dB and six of 24 P1 membership-sham margins were below 0.50 dB, although no sham beat G ([verdict:17–46](/D:/adags/research-wiki/operations/absfix-wave2-stage2-verdict-2026-09-15.md:17)).

The paper should say:

> “Under the frozen scene-wise conjunction requiring every G–U and G–sham pair to exceed 0.50 dB on both endpoints, Claim A was NOT_MET on both confirmatory scenes: one G–U ghost-core pair measured +0.44 dB, and six of 24 ghost-core membership-sham margins measured +0.23 to +0.43 dB. Post hoc, all 12 within-prefix G–U differences were positive on both the ghost-core and return endpoints, with medians of +2.40 and +2.49 dB. This directional consistency is descriptive and does not supersede the frozen verdict.”

That is sufficient for a workshop. It would still require a fresh preregistered scene or a better-powered hierarchical analysis for a main-track generalisation claim.

One additional statistical problem must be fixed: the 12 prefixes are independent training runs but are nested within only three scenes. The reported 97.5% interval is interpretable as a run-level conditional interval over these fixtures, not a scene-generalisation interval. Show every prefix grouped by scene, report scene medians, and avoid language implying 12 independent scene samples. The underlying values are at [absfix-stage2-report.md:116–169](/D:/adags/research-wiki/assets/absfix-stage2-report.md:116).

## Does sd = 1.59 dB undermine +2.40 dB?

It does **not** invalidate the +2.40 dB observed median or the 12/12 positive direction. It does invalidate precision-heavy wording.

The `n₂ = 269` calculation asks how many paired samples would be needed to resolve a much smaller **0.30 dB** effect under the measured 1.59 dB variability; it is not a power calculation for the observed 2.40 dB median ([report:329–347](/D:/adags/research-wiki/assets/absfix-stage2-report.md:329)). The right conclusion is:

- The gate produced a large conditional effect on these fixtures.
- Its magnitude is heterogeneous—especially between flame and cut-roasted-beef.
- Twelve runs do not estimate a 0.30 dB population effect precisely.
- The exact +2.40 number is descriptive, not a stable cross-scene effect size.

The greater problem is three-scene clustering, not that 1.59 exceeds 0.30.

## STG in the abstract

The current abstract sentence is too strong. STG uses six 50-frame models at 25k versus a single 300-frame 12k continuation; representation, capacity and pairing differ ([verdict:172–207](/D:/adags/research-wiki/operations/absfix-wave2-stage2-verdict-2026-09-15.md:172)).

It is admissible only with an explicit qualifier such as:

> “An unpaired published-schedule STG comparison suggests that temporal opacity makes the edited gap easy while weakening return; because representation, schedule, capacity and pairing differ, this is not a method ranking.”

I would omit the detailed numerical superiority claim from a 190-word abstract. Put the numbers in the main paper. “Better than any gated model” reads as an unfair benchmark claim.

## Q7: Is M2 constructible without CUDA changes?

**Yes—but not by relabelling rows, and not with the current lift left semantically unconstrained.**

Multi4D’s FG→TR lift reads foreground rows, creates transient rows, and leaves the donor FG rows in place: it is a copy, not a move ([code map:108–126](/D:/adags/research-wiki/operations/multi4d-code-map-2026-09-19.md:108)). The copied state includes target-specific appearance/opacity/geometry, while lineage is not currently carried automatically. A naïve “assign a fresh identity after the gap” control therefore leaks through:

1. the surviving pre-gap FG donor;
2. copied transient descendants;
3. canonical appearance and semantic features;
4. optimizer state, if episode tensors are reused;
5. potentially shared object-specific deformation state.

A valid M2 can still be implemented in Python/PyTorch because the proposed presence multiplication and gradient path occur before the CUDA rasteriser ([code map:128–150](/D:/adags/research-wiki/operations/multi4d-code-map-2026-09-19.md:128)). It needs:

- Identical episode slots, presence schedules and total parameter budget in M1 and M2.
- In M1, pre/post episode slots read the same persistent root memory.
- In M2, the post-gap slot reads a disjoint, freshly initialised root memory of identical dimension.
- No copied semantic, canonical-appearance, opacity or optimiser state from the pre-gap target.
- Explicit `parent_root_id` propagation through FG→TR lift, split, prune, capture and restore.
- M2 termination or zero-contribution of every pre-gap target root and descendant after the gap.
- Assertions that post-gap root IDs and ancestors are disjoint from the pre-gap target.
- A permutation/no-leak test showing that swapping identity associations changes the return identity result without changing visibility or capacity.

The lift creates a leak in the **naïve** M2. It does not make M2 impossible. The code map already identifies the lift as the highest-risk lineage drop ([code map:152–173](/D:/adags/research-wiki/operations/multi4d-code-map-2026-09-19.md:152)).

This is two-to-four days of implementation and testing, not a one-line control.

## Return-censored J&F

Return-censored J&F is a legitimate headline endpoint if the protocol is frozen and genuinely fair. The v0.3 conditions—pre-gap masks only, single pre-gap selection, no post-gap re-query, occlusion-aware rendering and fail-closed evaluation—are sound ([v0.3:116–148](/D:/adags/research-wiki/operations/ex4dgs-multi-interval-plan-v0.3-2026-09-21.md:116)).

It becomes self-serving if:

- the target is the only plausible object;
- there is no same-category or visually confusing distractor;
- post-gap pseudo-labels reach any method;
- cluster selection is repeated after return;
- missing frames are skipped;
- “re-ID” is claimed from segmentation alone.

The external baseline most likely to embarrass C is **Cutie**. It is a CVPR 2024 object-memory VOS method with public pretrained code; its standard script takes a first-frame mask and propagates without subsequent masks ([paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Cheng_Putting_the_Object_Back_into_Video_Object_Segmentation_CVPR_2024_paper.html), [official code](https://github.com/hkchengrex/Cutie)). It can be run per held-out camera with exactly the same last-pre-gap mask and no post-gap prompt. If Cutie matches or beats C on return J&F and swap rate, “persistent 4D memory” has not justified itself for the headline task.

Also include TRASE as the expected 4D segmentation comparator. Its training, Docker and evaluation code are now public ([TRASE repository](https://github.com/yunjinli/TRASE)), although adapting its supervision fairly is more work than running Cutie.

## Can authored-only C be CVPR main-track?

Practically, **no**. Not with PersistGS already occupying the object-permanence problem statement, not with only three related counterfactual fixtures, and not while the mechanism and semantic stage remain unproven. PersistGS must be treated as the closest conceptual prior, not a peripheral citation ([PersistGS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03479); local analysis at [literature check:144–168](/D:/adags/research-wiki/operations/literature-check-absence-return-2026-09-10.md:144)).

Minimum credible physical package:

- At least **two independently certified events in two different captured scenes**; three would be safer.
- At least one actual exit/out-of-view-and-return event and one all-camera physical occlusion-and-return event, labelled separately.
- Calibrated, synchronised views.
- Frozen all-view evidence that visible support is effectively zero during the gap.
- Independent pre/post identity association.
- At least one confusing distractor.
- Human-reviewable multi-camera montages plus depth/free-space evidence where available.
- The authored suite retained as controlled diagnostics, not the only positive evidence.

BEHAVE can provide a strong four-camera physical proof substrate, but it does not meet the programme’s ≥8-camera standard. It is supporting evidence, not a complete replacement. ImViD could satisfy the camera-count requirement if a valid event is found. Prior workspace memory says only Opera and Puppy are fully available and that their structural inventory is complete; I did not reverify an actual Class-A event in this review, so that remains unconfirmed.

Could this be obtained by November 16? Only if a usable event is identified by approximately September 25 and conversion begins immediately. If scouting fails or the event needs a new transfer/capture, no. By ICCV 2027, yes: a small physical benchmark plus the authored suite is a realistic target.

## Risk 1: the 2.7 GPU-hour check

Yes, it is the right first GPU move, but the montage alone is insufficient.

Run one M0 fixture reconstruction, then produce:

- full composite;
- FG-only;
- TR-only;
- alpha/contribution mass by target-root cohort;
- the same render with the candidate pre-gap root cohort causally disabled;
- gap, first-return and settled-return frames.

The key question is not merely “can I see an FG ghost?” It is:

> Do pre-gap persistent roots contribute incorrectly during the gap and correctly enough after return that suppressing their gap support while preserving their identity could improve both endpoints?

TR may visually cover a persistent-root error in the full composite. A full-image montage can therefore produce a false negative. The persistent/transient structure and temporal behaviour support this concern ([code map:12–18, 59–87](/D:/adags/research-wiki/operations/multi4d-code-map-2026-09-19.md:12)).
