## Confirmed defects

1. **CRITICAL — The setting argument omits a counterexample in its own experiment.** Recomputing the 18 wide-three-camera census files gives **0.8481% median / 1.6772% maximum** memory pixel-frames: the reported row matches. But the omitted **narrow-three-camera arm gives 1.0398% median**, exceeding the wide arm on the same rig. Both configurations are explicitly defined in [run_arc.sh, line 2](D:/adags-ars/fmd/run_arc.sh:2), with results in the [arc census directory](D:/adags-ars/fmd/census/arc). Wide spread reduces median never-seen area from **19.14% to 8.48%**, which could justify choosing it—but the stated explanation does not. **Fix:** report the narrow arm and choose geometry using memory coverage, never-seen coverage, and reconstruction feasibility jointly.

2. **MAJOR — “Exact census” becomes an unsupported universal conclusion.** [obs_census.py, lines 45–54](D:/adags-ars/fmd/obs_census.py:45) samples static surfaces on an eight-pixel grid in both dimensions. Exact intersection tests on those rays do not establish that dense benchmarks contain no memory failures, particularly on moving surfaces explicitly excluded here. Even the sampled dense arc is not literally empty: [ARS-L1-A0_d21_arc+44.json](D:/adags-ars/fmd/census/arc/ARS-L1-A0_d21_arc+44.json:14) contains **29** earlier-seen pixel-frames. **Fix:** restrict the conclusion to sampled static backgrounds in these fixtures; retain camera geometry and occluder placement as experimental variables.

3. **CRITICAL — The history labels and hidden-run statistic conflate different cases.** The expression `earlier = shows_static & ~seen & (cum_before > 0)` does **not** exclude future sightings ([line 111](D:/adags-ars/fmd/obs_census.py:111)). Thus “seen only earlier” includes points seen both before and after the gap. For offline reconstruction, those future observations matter. Separately, `hid = shows_static[f] & ~seen[f]` counts never-seen and later-only points in hidden runs ([lines 125–140](D:/adags-ars/fmd/obs_census.py:125)). One output reports **2,258** pixels with runs ≥10 frames but only **520** pixels ever classified as memory ([output, lines 25–28](D:/adags-ars/fmd/census/arc/ARS-L1-A0_c3w_arc+44.json:25)). **Fix:** distinguish past-only, future-only, and past-and-future; compute memory-episode durations separately from unobserved runs.

4. **MAJOR — The measurement contract contradicts its thresholds and overstates cancellation.** The defined PSNR difference-of-differences becomes **negative** when memory quality deteriorates, whereas H1/H4 require a deficit “of at least 3 dB” ([definition](D:/adags-ars/fmd/review/request_rev1.md:168)). Moreover, subtraction cancels common additive effects only under appropriate matching and parallel trends; it does not automatically cancel surface difficulty or run quality. **Fix:** define a positive degradation score, specify surface correspondence and pre-event visibility, test control pre-trends, and estimate uncertainty for this endpoint rather than borrowing whole-frame noise floors.

5. **MAJOR — The common evaluator does not isolate history classes for LPIPS.** [eval_event_windows.py, lines 150–190](D:/adags/scripts/eval_event_windows.py:150) converts a mask into a padded bounding rectangle and computes LPIPS over that rectangle. The mask itself is not supplied to LPIPS ([lines 255–257](D:/adags/scripts/eval_event_windows.py:255)). Scattered memory pixels can therefore produce an almost whole-frame score containing seen-now and never-seen regions. **Fix:** implement an explicitly masked perceptual metric, or label this contextual crop LPIPS and keep masked pixel error primary.

6. **MAJOR — H3’s numbers match, but they do not establish its mechanism.** Recomputing Multi4D’s nine score files gives **3.172 / 8.016 / 10.693 dB**, with **7/9 ≥5 dB**—matching the table. The bottle frame-90 value is **23.991 dB** ([score](D:/adags-ars/wave1/scores/multi4d/ars_w1_ARS-L1-A0_multi4d_rep1.json:14283)). However, the fixture explicitly says **“target removed from the world”** ([event specification](D:/adags-ars/wave1/ARS-L1-A0/event_spec.json:17)); its presence resumes at frame 90 ([line 2372](D:/adags-ars/wave1/ARS-L1-A0/event_spec.json:2372)). This is an artificial existence discontinuity, not yet an occluder-only reveal. Training-view error would also remain compatible with optimization failure. **Fix:** run the genuine re-reveal control and a temporal-support intervention before asserting representation incapacity. RetimeGS explicitly describes excessively narrow Ex4DGS temporal falloffs, undermining “smooth implies cannot switch within one frame.” [RetimeGS, Appendix C.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13783v1)

7. **MAJOR — Promotion does not test all the hypotheses being promoted.** H1 claims worsening with hidden duration, but promotion requires only a deficit after 16 frames. H4 claims superiority of canonical deformation models, but its first test and criterion compare two banks within Multi4D ([H4](D:/adags-ars/fmd/review/request_rev1.md:236)). Bank-only renders remove complementary geometry and occlusion, so their PSNR difference is not an architecture comparison. **Fix:** require an age-effect test for H1 and an actual canonical-model comparison plus controlled routing intervention for H4.

8. **CRITICAL — The fallback defeats the scientific stop rules.** “If nothing is promoted … commit” ([line 278](D:/adags-ars/fmd/review/request_rev1.md:278)) permits commitment despite failed effect, mechanism, novelty, or real-footage requirements. Intermediate effects between kill and promote thresholds also have no explicit disposition, and H1’s clock starts only after runs finish. **Fix:** use absolute deadlines; expire unresolved lanes from the current submission; permit “no admissible CVPR claim.” A dense fallback must pass its own gates.

## Suspected issues

- **MAJOR — H1/H2 may measure geometry or optimization failure rather than forgetting or absence misclassification.** Bank decomposition is descriptive evidence. Settle this with matched temporal exposure, initialization controls, and interventions on opacity support, routing, and occluder depth.

- **MAJOR — Real moving-surface history is underspecified.** Dense-model depth permits same-time visibility tests; it does not supply temporal identity through motion. Synthetic agreement cannot establish real-data accuracy. Require independently checked tracks, class-specific precision/recall, retained coverage, and uncertainty versus gap duration.

- **MAJOR — The schedule assumes substantial adapter work is routine.** The inspected public Multi4D loader hardcodes **300 frames**, rejects other counts, uses one focal value, and excludes the evaluation camera. The proposed Panoptic sequences have 150 frames. A “three-camera tree” also needs explicit accounting to ensure three cameras actually train. Verify the pinned implementation and adapter before costing runs. [Upstream loader](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BatFaceWayne/Multi4D/main/scene/neural_3D_dataset_NDC.py)
