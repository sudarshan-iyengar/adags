# FROZEN PLAN (NOT LAUNCHED) — N3V flow/depth supervision matrix

Date: 2026-08-17. Status: **PLAN ONLY. NOTHING SUBMITTED.**
Gate: this matrix may not launch until experiment 123
(hull + extended exposure) has completed and been interpreted.

Purpose: test the two most consequential ImViD ideas — rendered optical
flow supervision and aligned monocular depth supervision — **without**
changing dataset, initialization, supervision and representation at the
same time. Every cell below shares one dataset, one split, one
initialization, one exposure, one capacity and one hardware class.

EXPLORATORY. Nothing here is claim-grade.

## Why N3V, decided on evidence rather than preference

The directive prefers N3V "unless primary evidence establishes that the
required targets and evaluation are equally reliable on scissor or
ImViD". It does not.

* **N3V has real flow on disk for ALL SIX scenes**: `<scene>/flow/`,
  `camNN_FFFF.npz` with keys exactly `{flow, mask}`, generated by
  SEA-RAFT (`scripts/run_phase9_depth_visibility.py` seals them and
  refuses any record whose key set differs). `cut_roasted_beef` alone is
  5,980 files / 56.28 GiB. Verified by listing, not assumed.
* **DiVa-360 scissor has NO flow and no depth of any kind.**
* **ImViD ships neither flow nor depth nor masks nor points** — its own
  baseline computes flow and depth at training time from RGB.

So N3V is the only substrate where the flow cell can be run from
existing, audited targets rather than from targets this project would
first have to manufacture.

**Scene: `cut_roasted_beef`.** It is the approved development scene, and
— decisively — it is the ONLY scene whose flow has been through the
Stage-1/1B reliability scrutiny. The other five have equivalent files
and NO equivalent audit.

## The trap this plan exists to avoid

`enable_rendered_flow` and `lambda_track_flow` are INDEPENDENT config
keys and **both** must be set for the flow loss to supervise anything.

`gaussian_renderer/__init__.py:295-301` computes the per-Gaussian 2D
displacement only under `pc.enable_rendered_flow`; otherwise `flow_2d`
is an exact-zero tensor. `main.py:1320-1330` applies the flow loss
whenever `lambda_track_flow > 0`, with no check on the other key.

**Setting `lambda_track_flow > 0` alone therefore trains a constant-zero
prediction toward real flow targets** — a loss that penalizes the model
for the renderer being switched off, gradient-free with respect to
motion. It would look like "flow supervision hurt". Both keys are set
together in every flow cell here, and the control asserts both are off.

Neither key has EVER been set in any tracked config or any recorded run
([[gap_map]] records the hook as an open gap). So this matrix is the
first activation of that path, and it starts from a known-zero baseline.

## The fifth cell the evidence demands

The directive names four cells. This plan adds a **fifth**, and the
reason is binding negative evidence from this project's own history.

[[operations/phase9-csvl-vpl-stage1-result]]: on `cut_roasted_beef`,
using these same SEA-RAFT sidecars, **camera-swapped flow scored BETTER
than valid flow** (mean confidence 0.922 vs 0.889, EPE 1.010 vs 1.418),
and [[operations/phase9-csvl-vpl-stage1b-result]] found removing flow
changed only 3 of 206 selected edges (98.565% overlap) while valid flow
*increased* endpoint error against zero flow on 78.98% of candidates.

That evidence is about flow as an ASSOCIATION cue in the retired P03
pipeline, not as a photometric training loss, and it is NOT a refutation
of the loss this matrix tests. But it establishes that on this exact
data a MIS-REGISTERED flow field can outscore the correct one. **A flow
win with no swapped control is therefore not attributable.** The
`lane_l5_shifted` config is the existing precedent for exactly this kind
of control.

|---|---|---|---|---|

## Development split — carved, never the shipped holdout

N3V's shipped split is **test = `cam00` only**; train = `cam01`-`cam20`
excluding `cam04`. One held-out camera is far too thin to rank five
cells on, and ranking on it would repeat exactly the failure the DiVa
sweep caught ([[operations/diva360-scissor-sweep-matrix-v1]]: a +0.909 dB
development win that reversed on the official split).

So: **rank on a development split carved from the TRAINING cameras**,
leaving `cam00` sealed. The carve is by camera id, frozen before any
cell runs, and `cam00` never appears in any training or ranking input.
`cam00` is scored EXACTLY ONCE, on the winner, at the end.

PROHIBITED HELD-OUT INFORMATION, explicitly: `cam00` imagery, `cam00`
flow sidecars, `cam00` masks, and any metric computed on `cam00` may not
influence cell selection, loss weights, stopping, or any hyperparameter.

## Flow: source, masks, and what is NOT assumed

* Source: the existing sealed SEA-RAFT `flow/camNN_FFFF.npz`.
  `MotionPriorCache._candidate_roots` falls back to `source_path`, so
  these are discovered with **zero code changes**.
* Validity mask: the npz's own `mask` key, intersected with the
  trainer's `dynamic_mask` where present (`main.py:1320-1330`). Where no
  mask key exists the loader falls back to `isfinite(flow)`.
* `motion_track_dt`: N3V is 30 FPS, and the default `0.0333333333` is
  1/30. It must MATCH the flow sidecars' temporal step; the sidecars are
  frame-to-frame, so 1/30 is correct here and is asserted, not assumed.
* **UNVERIFIED and gating**: the exact units and direction convention of
  the npz `flow` arrays (pixels vs normalized; `t -> t+1` vs reverse),
  and their resolution (Stage-1B recorded 1352x1014, which is NOT the
  half-res training raster). A one-cell smoke must confirm the loss is
  finite, the mask is non-degenerate, and the resize path is correct
  BEFORE the matrix runs. A sign or scale error here is indistinguishable
  from "flow supervision hurts".

## Depth: this cell is GREENFIELD and is the gating work item

There is **no depth supervision anywhere in this repository**: no config
key, no loss, no alignment code. `render_pkg["depth"]` is unpacked at
`main.py:1274` and never used again; the rasterizer also computes
`invdepth` and it is DISCARDED before `render_pkg` is built; the two
`Ldepth` TensorBoard stubs are dead because `Ldepth` is never assigned.

So S-D and S-FD require, before they can run:

1. a depth target — DA3 monocular depth is available in this project's
   depth stack but is NOT currently produced per training view;
2. **alignment** — no scale/shift fitting code exists. The
   `depth_visibility` module locks `scale_shift_fit: False` and fails
   closed on any override, so that constant may NOT simply be flipped;
   an aligner for the trainer is new code with its own review;
3. a loss, a config key, and the render buffer plumbed through.

Monocular depth is scale- and shift-ambiguous. Supervising a metric
scene with unaligned relative depth is not a weaker version of the idea,
it is a different and wrong one. **S-D and S-FD are therefore blocked on
that implementation and a focused review of the alignment rule.** S-C,
S-F and S-X are not blocked and can run first as a three-cell subset,
reported as a subset.

## Loss weights — derived here, never copied

ImViD reports λ_depth = 1.0 and λ_flow = 1.0. **Those numbers are not
transferable and are not used.** ImViD's flow term is an endpoint error
on a rasterized velocity field; this repository's is
`compute_flow_loss` = masked L1 on a resized pixel-displacement map
(`main.py:110-117`). Different quantity, different units, different
normalization — an identical λ means nothing across them.

Weights are instead set so each new term's INITIAL magnitude is a
declared fraction of the photometric loss at iteration 0, measured on
one smoke run per term and then FROZEN before the matrix. The fraction
is declared in advance (target: the new term contributes ~5-10% of the
total at initialization) and the resulting λ is recorded as a measured
constant, not tuned against any metric.

## Evaluation

Development-split PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS for ranking. The winner is scored ONCE
on `cam00` with the official-convention evaluator
(`scripts/eval_diva360_heldout.py`'s metric module is dataset-agnostic;
its DiVa-specific split logic is not). BOTH metric conventions are
reported and never averaged, per
[[operations/diva360-protocol-parity-audit]] — and the convention deltas
are MEASURED per model, since they were shown there to vary with the
quality regime rather than being constants.

## Failure criteria, declared in advance

* **Any cell whose flow loss is non-finite, or whose flow mask covers
  <1% or >99% of pixels, is a PLUMBING FAILURE**, not a scientific
  result, and is fixed and re-run rather than reported as a negative.
* **If S-X (camera-swapped flow) matches or beats S-F**, the flow result
  is UNATTRIBUTABLE and no flow claim may be made — exactly the Stage-1
  outcome, reproduced in the training loss. This is the single most
  important stopping rule in the plan.
* **If S-F differs from S-C by less than the control's own run-to-run
  spread**, the honest statement is "no measured effect", not a small
  win. That spread is unknown here because seed variance has never been
  measured on this config; a second S-C at a different seed is the
  cheapest way to bound it and is included.
* A cell that OOMs or diverges is preserved and reported, never silently
  re-run with changed settings.

## What this matrix cannot establish

That flow or depth supervision helps EL-GS; that any result transfers to
DiVa-360 or ImViD; that N3V flow quality generalizes beyond
`cut_roasted_beef`, the only scene with a flow audit; or anything about
the temporal representation, which is untouched here.
