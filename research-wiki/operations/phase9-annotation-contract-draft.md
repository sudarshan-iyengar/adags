## Purpose

Human-audited disocclusion event tracks are the claim-grade Gate A reference
and the event-evaluation ground truth for Gate B. They validate, and are never
an input to, the E1 per-primitive visibility states and E2 reveal detections.
This is the schedule-critical path: A05/A06 have been `not_evaluable` since
2026-07-15, and no claim-grade Gate A or Gate B verdict is possible without
these labels.

## Unit of annotation: the event track

One event track is one hidden-surface episode of one physical surface region:

- `scene`, `camera` (annotation camera is a training camera; cam00 is never
  annotated or opened for this purpose);
- `t_start .. t_end` frame range covering pre-occlusion visibility, the
  occluded interval, and at least 5 frames after reveal;
- per-frame state of the tracked surface region:
  `visible | occluded | partially_occluded | uncertain`;
- occluder identity (free text: hand / knife / tongs / torch / spoon / body);
- polygon boundary of the tracked surface region at three keyframes minimum
  (last fully visible frame, first fully revealed frame, one mid-episode
  frame if partially visible), at native evaluation resolution;
- ordering assertion: at occlusion onset, which surface is in front
  (occluder) and which behind (tracked region), with an
  `uncertain_ordering` escape;
- annotator confidence per field: `high | medium | low`;
- `notes` free text.

Abstention is a first-class label: annotators must mark `uncertain` rather
than guess. Uncertain frames count as misses for method recall (contract
Section 7) — the reference must not be diluted to make methods look good.

## Counts and stratification (contract-fixed)

- At least 24 tracks total; target 30-36.
- Stratified across `cut_roasted_beef`, `flame_steak`, `sear_steak`
  (approximately equal thirds), with only the `cut_roasted_beef` portion
  exposed during development. `flame_steak`/`sear_steak` tracks are produced
  and then sealed until method freeze.
- Event families to cover per scene where present: hand-over-food,
  utensil-over-food (knife/tongs/spoon), torch/flame occlusion,
  body-over-counter. No family completely absent if it occurs in the scene.
- Track selection is made from a candidate list generated by a label-free
  procedure (uniform temporal stratification plus an optional E1-census
  candidate list marked as such); the selector documents why each candidate
  was accepted or rejected BEFORE any method scores on those windows are
  seen. The historical R009 windows are excluded from selection entirely.

## Double annotation and adjudication (contract-fixed)

- At least 20% of tracks (minimum 6 at target counts) are selected for
  independent double annotation BEFORE any labels are produced; the selection
  is recorded in this page's follow-up.
- Report per-field agreement: state-per-frame agreement, boundary IoU at the
  4 px tolerance, ordering agreement, onset/reveal frame deltas.
- Disagreements are adjudicated under this frozen rubric:
  1. state disagreements at episode boundaries resolve to the later onset /
     earlier reveal (conservative shorter occlusion);
  2. boundary disagreements resolve to the intersection polygon if IoU >= 0.5,
     otherwise the track field is marked `uncertain`;
  3. ordering disagreements resolve to `uncertain_ordering`;
  4. no adjudication may consult any method output.

## Tooling

- CVAT, using the existing repository support
  (`depth_visibility/cvat_annotation.py` and `depth_visibility/annotation.py`
  packet/schema code) for packet export and label import validation.
- Annotation packets contain RGB frames of the annotation camera only, with
  frame indices; no depth, no model renders, no census output, no R009
  metadata inside the packet.
- Import validation is fail-closed: schema, frame-range, camera-identity, and
  target-exclusion checks must pass before labels are sealed.

## Discipline (binding)

- Labels are evaluation-only: never an input, loss, mask, threshold source,
  or selection cue for any method lane.
- `flame_steak`/`sear_steak` labels are sealed at creation (hash recorded,
  content unopened) until method freeze; a single claim-grade evaluation
  consumes them.
- R009 windows are excluded from candidate selection, packets, and rubric
  calibration; they remain historical continuity only.
- Annotator identities and time spent are recorded for the paper's
  reproducibility statement.

## Open decisions for the user

1. **Who annotates.** Options: (a) the user plus one independent colleague
   (satisfies independence for the double-annotated subset); (b) two
   independent colleagues; (c) a paid annotation service with the rubric.
   The contract requires genuine human labels; agents cannot produce them.
2. **Candidate-list source.** Pure label-free temporal stratification, or
   stratification plus the E1 census candidate list (flagged per candidate)?
   The second is more efficient but must be disclosed in the paper as
   census-assisted selection.
3. **Annotation resolution.** Native 1352x1014 or the eval resolution
   676x507 (resolution: 2)? Boundary tolerances (2/4/8 px) are defined at
   native evaluation resolution in the contract; recommend annotating at
   native and downsampling.
4. **Timeline.** Estimated 20-40 minutes per track including double
   annotation overhead: roughly 12-24 annotator-hours for 30-36 tracks.
   When should this be scheduled? It gates Phase 3/4 claim-grade evaluation,
   not Phase 1-2 engineering.
