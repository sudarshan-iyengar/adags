## 1. The deferral, decided on arithmetic before any lane result was read

Measured: the 300-frame capped arm took **15.85 slot-h for 36,000
iterations**, at ~1.08 s/it before densification and rising with point
count; the 600k cap saturates by iteration 6,000, so per-iteration cost
is near-constant thereafter. Decomposing the measured total gives
≈1.83 h for the first 6,000 iterations and ≈1.68 s/it afterwards, so a
**12,000-iteration cell costs ≈ 4.6 slot-h**.

The design below requires **four training cells** (2 arms × 2 paired
seeds) ≈ **18.5 slot-h**, plus four 300-frame evaluations ≈ 2.4 slot-h,
for **≈ 21 slot-h against a 24 slot-h block ceiling**. That displaces
the payload and timing lanes, which are the paper blockers, and the
promotion conditions forbid exactly that displacement. Amendment rule 8
forbids dropping to one seed to make it fit.

**Therefore the comparison defers whole, and is frozen here so it is
launch-ready under a future budget.**

**A 6,000-iteration variant was considered and REJECTED**, though it
would have fit (≈10 slot-h). Both 300-frame arms are measured to peak
near 12,000 iterations; at 6,000 the capped model reads 32.902 against
33.508 at 12,000. A 6k comparison would measure the operator at a
pre-peak transient with each unit seeing only ~2.1 presentations, not at
the frozen endpoint the question is about. Recorded as a rejected
alternative rather than silently omitted.

## 2. Why all four cells are genuinely required

**B0-C cannot substitute for B0-R.** Verified: `b0c_canonical_crb300.yaml`
contains no `elgs_reserved_parity` line and therefore trained on ALL
training units, while the ladder arms set `elgs_reserved_parity: true`
and train on ~75% of them. The same caveat is already recorded for the
50-frame ladder, whose cells are explicitly NOT comparable to
experiments 181/194 for this reason. Reusing B0-C's 33.251 as the
control for a reserved-parity B1 would confound the operator with a
~25% change in training-unit supply.

## 4. Packet-birth schedule — derivation and its two disclosed deviations

The 50-frame ladder B1 operator is `interval 500`, `fraction 0.005`,
`t_sigma_frames 1.5`, window `[1000, 4000]` inside a 6,000-iteration
schedule — 7 events, ~3.3% of rows relocated.

**Frozen for 300 frames at 12,000 iterations: `interval 500`,
`fraction 0.005`, `t_sigma_frames 1.5`, window `[2000, 8000]` → 13
events.**

The invariant deliberately held fixed is the OPERATOR ITSELF — the
per-event donor fraction, the temporal sigma, and the cadence rule are
what the name "B1" denotes, and they are unchanged. The window is placed
at the same PROPORTIONAL position as the 50-frame cell (17%→67% of the
schedule), which also preserves the post-birth training budget at 33% of
the schedule so relocated rows get proportionally the same opportunity
to train.

Two consequences follow and are disclosed rather than smoothed:

* **temporal coverage is ~3× sparser per frame.** Each birth event acts
  at ONE training timestamp, so 13 events sample 13 of 300 frames where
  the 50-frame cell sampled 7 of 50.
* **cumulative relocated fraction is ~1.9× larger** (13 × 0.5% ≈ 6.5%
  against ~3.3%).

These cannot both be matched while holding the operator fixed. A
coverage-matched variant (~42 events) or a budget-matched variant (7
events at `interval 1000`) is a **DIFFERENT frozen specification**, not
a tuning of this one, and would need its own registration.

**The endpoint is CLASS-SPLIT and this is a scientific decision, not
bookkeeping.** B1 relocates capacity into DYNAMIC-MASK regions, so it
has no mechanism by which it would act on a static background reveal.
Pooling both classes would dilute the quantity the operator moves and
let a background result masquerade as an event result. Primary endpoint
= the `dynamic_content` class; `static_background` is reported
separately as a secondary diagnostic. The tool's own
`all_events_union` mixes classes and is NOT the primary endpoint;
class-pooled PSNR is recovered exactly from the per-event outputs as
`pooled_mse = Σ_e n_e · 10^(−psnr_e/10) / Σ_e n_e`, `n_e = 3 ·
pixel_times_e`.

**Two findings from the curation bind this comparison:**

* **The clip contains essentially ONE large, clean occlude-and-return
  event on dynamic content** — `F`, the blade across the beef pile.
  With `A`/`B`/`C` excluded as unconfirmed, the primary endpoint rests
  on a single event of 20 scored view-frames. That is a real limit on
  what this comparison can establish, and it is a scene fact rather
  than a design choice.
* **The frozen 0-49 dev masks are not confirmed by ground truth** —
  `B`'s and `C`'s `[34,39]` interval appears to label the
  occluder-PRESENT window. No recorded number is retracted, but the
  reading of the ladder's +0.077/+0.345 as an event-region effect is
  weakened, because those masks score mostly static pixels.

## 6. Gates, frozen before any output

Every threshold traces to a measured prior quantity; none is invented.

1. **Event-region primary (replication test):** the event-ray union
   delta `B1 − B0-R` must be **≥ 0 on BOTH seeds** with a **positive
   paired mean**. This is the exact shape of the 50-frame observation
   being tested for survival (+0.077 / +0.345, both seeds positive).
2. **Per-segment reporting:** each frozen temporal segment is reported
   separately with its own endpoint. **No promotion may rest on a single
   segment.**
3. **Complement non-harm:** paired mean complement delta **≥ −0.10 dB**
   (the existing CCR static-region bound).
4. **Global non-catastrophe:** neither seed's global PSNR delta may fall
   below **−0.30 dB**, derived from the measured 50-frame B0 seed spread
   of ±0.28 dB and rounded outward.
5. **Capacity parity:** the 600k cap must bind in both arms and realized
   point counts must agree to within the ladder's observed ~100-row
   band.
6. Aggregation is the mean of paired deltas; a single-seed result
   licenses nothing — the 50-frame ladder measured per-seed deltas of
   −0.271 and +0.292 on a globally neutral operator, so either seed
   alone would have been wrong.

## 7. Unknown that this design does not remove

The **300-frame seed spread has never been measured** — the ±0.28 dB
figure is the 50-frame B0 spread, and gate 4 borrows it. A 300-frame
model has a different variance structure (per-frame spread was measured
at 0.73 dB within a single model). Gate 4 is therefore a borrowed and
possibly optimistic bound, and is labelled as such rather than presented
as calibrated. The two paired seeds this design runs are what would
begin to measure it.
