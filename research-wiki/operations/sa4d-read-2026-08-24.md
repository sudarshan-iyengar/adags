## 2. The mechanism — verified against the primary text

Every quotation below was re-verified by the primary agent against the
cached full text, not taken on report.

* **Backbone: a DEFORMATION FIELD, with NO per-primitive temporal
  support.** 4D-GS — one canonical Gaussian set plus a HexPlane encoder and
  MLP heads deforming **position, rotation and scale only**. Opacity and
  colour are time-invariant, so **every primitive contributes at every
  timestamp**. The paper rejects our substrate family explicitly: *"unlike
  other representations … that focus only on local temporal spaces."*
* **Identity IS time-varying — and it is a FIELD, not a per-primitive
  parameter.** *"time-variant Identity features `e` for each Gaussian"*,
  32-d, produced by a global MLP queried at (canonical position, time).
  *"Instead of attaching a time-invariant feature vector to each Gaussian
  as most 3D segmentation methods do, we introduce a Temporal Identity
  Feature Field Network (TFF) … **to solve the Gaussian drifting**."*
  **The motivation is decisive: time-variance exists to fix a row changing
  owner mid-sequence — a defect of the deformation substrate — NOT to model
  presence.**
* **Supervision: purely 2D, and single-camera on N3V.** DEVA masks, 2D
  cross-entropy, plus Gaussian Grouping's kNN regularizer ported with
  identical hyperparameters. On N3V they train on **one camera only**,
  because DEVA assigns the same object different ids in different camera
  videos.
* **No densification, because the Gaussians are FROZEN:** *"freezing the
  weight of ℱ and 𝒢"* during identity training. **`densif`, `clone` and
  `prune` occur ZERO times** in the full text — verified by count.
* **Metrics: rendered-2D only.** *"calculate the IoU and Accuracy"* on
  20-30 hand-annotated 2D frames per scene. **No per-primitive 3D
  precision, recall or IoU. "PSNR" occurs ZERO times.**
* **Abstention: none per primitive.** Softmax over C classes, no threshold,
  no reject class. The "void" class is a 2D pixel label from DEVA.
* **Suppression: implemented, but GLOBAL IN TIME.** A Gaussian identity
  table indexed `(g, t)` means a row can be removed at some timestamps and
  kept at others — but what varies is **membership, not presence**; every
  object is assumed present at every timestamp; and the edit removes the
  object for the whole sequence. **`absence`, `reappear` and `lifespan`
  occur ZERO times** — verified by count.
* **Nothing is inferred.** Every temporal quantity is either a conditioning
  input to a label-supervised MLP or supplied per-frame by DEVA. *(A
  near-miss worth recording: "interval" appears four times and always means
  the refinement's timestamp STRIDE — a compute knob, easily misread as a
  temporal window.)*

## 5. One idea worth taking, aimed squarely at a measured ADAGS failure

**Membership as a TIME-CONDITIONED FIELD rather than a label bound once at
seeding.** This block measured that ADAGS binds membership once at
`create_from_pcd` and that a 4.5%-precision seed is then **amplified** by
densification into 4,317 of 4,576 rows. **A field re-decided per frame
cannot be amplified that way** — the failure mode is structurally absent.

Also transferable, and stated by the authors with a reason: the **two-stage
freeze** — train photometry first, then learn membership with the geometry
frozen — because joint semantic training degrades novel-view quality. That
bears on any ADAGS design that would learn membership jointly.

## 6. Three hazards to carry

1. **Total assignment with no abstention** is the same active hazard as
   SGG's, given the measured ordering partially gated **24.67** << not gated
   **27.14** < fully gated **28.19**.
2. **A refinement step that deleted an entire object from a single
   mislabelled supervision frame** — documented by the authors themselves
   (a cup labelled "void" at t=0, *"the whole cup is removed after
   refinement"*). SGG has no equivalent failure.
3. **The identity field is a smooth interpolant in `t`** — positionally
   encoded, continuous — which is the **wrong prior for the step
   discontinuity** ADAGS is trying to detect, and it is never tested on one.

### A CORRECTION to §2 and §3 of this page

Those sections head the suppression row **"GLOBAL IN TIME"**. **That is
wrong, and the body of §2 already contradicted the header** by noting a row
can be removed at some timestamps and kept at others.

**Verified in code:** `create_mask_table` builds
`torch.zeros((num_sample, N)).cuda().bool()` — a **`[T, N]` boolean stack,
one row per training timestamp** — and each row is computed
**independently**, with no temporal-consistency term, no smoothing and no
hysteresis. A refinement pass makes it *more* per-frame still, pruning each
row by that frame's own 2D projection, so a Gaussian occluded at frame `t`
is dropped from row `t` alone.

**The accurate statement is therefore NOT "global in time" but "per-frame
and unstructured":** removal genuinely varies per timestamp; what is absent
is any **interval** representation. An exhaustive grep over ~25 semantic
terms (`t_start`, `lifespan`, `birth`, `time_window`, `presence`,
`disappear`, `reappear`, `dormant`, …) returned **only wall-clock timing
hits**. There is one time-collapsing path, and only for export:
`save_seg_gs` writes the object PLY using `mask_table.any(dim=0)`, the
union over all time.

**This makes the cell ADAGS occupies sharper, not blurrier.** The
difference is no longer "per-timestamp vs global" — SA4D *is*
per-timestamp. It is **an unstructured stack of independent per-frame
decisions versus a per-primitive INTERVAL that is inferred.**

### Three UNDETERMINED items, now resolved

* **Removal mechanism: RENDER-TIME MASKING.** `means3D = pc._xyz[mask]` —
  a boolean gather onto a copy. Nothing is deleted and no opacity is
  zeroed; the model tensors are untouched. Deletion is "pass the
  complement".
* **Identity storage: an MLP, with NO per-Gaussian identity array.**
  `SegNet.forward(self, point, time)`, 4 layers, width 256, sinusoidal
  encodings, queried at the **canonical** position rather than the deformed
  one. The per-Gaussian alternative is commented out in the source, and
  only `mlp.pt` / `classifier.pt` are ever saved.
* **The "freeze" is implemented BY OMISSION, not by assertion.** No
  `requires_grad=False` appears anywhere; the Gaussian attributes are
  simply absent from the identity stage's optimizer parameter groups.
  Gradients still flow into them and are never stepped. The paper's claim
  holds in effect.

### Two findings that STRENGTHEN this page's existing claims

* **Densification during identity training: ZERO** — verified by count in
  `train_ie.py`. Confirms §2 from the other side.
* **No 3D or per-Gaussian metric exists in the codebase at all.**
  `metrics.py` — the only standalone evaluation script — computes **only**
  PSNR, SSIM, MS-SSIM, D-SSIM and LPIPS; a grep for
  `iou|jaccard|dice|precision|recall` returns **zero**. Segmentation
  evaluation lives only in notebook cells and is 2D mIoU/mAcc. **And that
  cell scores a single collapsed `final_mask3d`, not the per-timestamp
  table — so even the 2D number never evaluates the table's temporal
  dimension.** The per-primitive-metrics finding is now at its strongest:
  the one method that actually *builds* a per-primitive per-timestamp
  membership table scores it neither per-primitive nor per-timestamp.
* **Abstention: none, and `prob_thresh` is its OPPOSITE.** Assignment is an
  unconditional `argmax` over 256 classes. `prob_thresh` (as low as 0.001)
  is unioned with the argmax result, so it **widens** membership rather
  than gating it.
